Features

Everything You Need,
Nothing You Don't.

PClinic combines powerful patient management with a beautiful, intuitive interface that works offline.

📋

Patient Records

Store complete patient profiles with ID, age, contact, medical history, and diagnosis all in one place.

📸

Media Attachments

Capture and attach photos and videos directly to patient records. Perfect for X-rays, wounds, and progress tracking.

🔍

Smart Search

Find any patient instantly by name, ID, diagnosis, hospital, or even clinical notes.

Multi-Language

Full support for English, French, and Kinyarwanda. Perfect for clinics in East Africa and beyond.

Advanced Reports

Generate professional reports in PDF, Word, or CSV. Filter by hospital, diagnosis, date range, and more.
