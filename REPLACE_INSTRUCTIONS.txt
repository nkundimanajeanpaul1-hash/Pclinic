IMPORTANT — deploy functions by name only when you mean to limit the deploy.
The previous version of this file listed only the three laboratory callables
(labSpecimenTransition, labFinalize, labAcknowledgeCritical). Anyone following
it deploys the laboratory and silently leaves the five radiology callables
undeployed, which is exactly what happened on pclinic-20d81 on 2026-08-28:

    https://africa-south1-pclinic-20d81.cloudfunctions.net/labSpecimenTransition
        -> 401 {"error":{"message":"Sign-in is required.","status":"UNAUTHENTICATED"}}   (deployed)
    .../radiologyTransition            -> 404 "Page not found"   (never deployed)
    .../radiologySaveDraft             -> 404   (never deployed)
    .../radiologyFinalize              -> 404   (never deployed)
    .../radiologyAddendum              -> 404   (never deployed)
    .../radiologyAcknowledgeCritical   -> 404   (never deployed)

With radiologyTransition missing, "Start study" cannot move a study out of
pending, so no report can be acquired, written or signed. `firebase deploy
--only functions` deploys all eight and is the safe default.

Do not deploy hosting alone. labSpecimenTransition and labFinalize are required
to safely accession, reject and release recovered legacy laboratory requests that
have no /orders document; the radiology callables are required for every imaging
transition, draft, signature, addendum and critical acknowledgement.
After deployment, hard-refresh the laboratory and radiology dashboards before
retesting, and confirm in Cloud Run that a service exists for each name above.
