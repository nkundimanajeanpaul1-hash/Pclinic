PClinic — Update package v2 (2026-09-02): "Open DICOM viewer" button → PClinic DICOM Viewer page
================================================================================================

Extract this zip into your Pclinic project folder (the one containing
radio-dashboard.html). It overwrites the 6 files below.

WHAT THE BUTTON DOES NOW
------------------------
Radiology action bar → "Open DICOM viewer" (next to "Add radiology result"):
  • No patient selected  → button is locked; click = "Select a patient first" + patient picker.
  • Patient selected     → the PClinic DICOM Viewer page ALWAYS opens (full screen):
      - left "DICOM Explorer" lists the patient and EVERY one of their studies
        (open ones first, then reported; cancelled excluded);
      - the first study is displayed; click another study in the explorer to switch;
      - Upload (top-right) attaches JPEG/PNG/WebP/GIF/MP4/WebM/DICOM to the displayed study;
      - patient with NO imaging study → the viewer still opens, empty, and explains why
        (Upload is disabled with the same explanation).
  • Never changes workflow state, never jumps to the report writer.
  • Esc or Close closes it. Re-clicking while open replaces the viewer (no stacking).
