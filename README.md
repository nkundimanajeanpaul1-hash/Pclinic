#PClinic – Clinical Management
PClinic  helps doctors, nurses, and clinic staff manage patient files on a phone or tablet—even without internet.
Main features

Create and manage patient records (name, age, ID, phone, hospital)
Clinical notes, diagnosis, and intervention/treatment plans
Add photos and videos to each patient file (camera or gallery)
Search, filter, and sort the patient queue
Statistics dashboard (by hospital, diagnosis, and time)
Print single patient records or full reports
Export reports to Word, CSV, or PDF-style print
Share or import individual patient data (JSON)
Full backup with photos/videos (download + optional Google Drive upload)
Light email backup for text data (restore after reinstall)
Works offline — data stays on the device
Multi-language: English, French, Kinyarwanda
Secure login with optional recovery code
Dark / light theme

Who it’s for
Orthopedic and general clinics, hospital wards, and outreach teams who need fast, simple patient documentation without depending on hospital Wi‑Fi.
Data & privacy
Patient data is stored on the device. Backups are only created when the user exports a file, uploads to Drive, or sends an email backup.
